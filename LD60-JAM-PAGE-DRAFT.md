## Long description

**MECHA PROTOCOL** is a turn-based mecha tactics game built in 72 hours.

You command a lance of customizable battle mechs on a 10×8 grid. There's no dice between you and victory — every enemy attack is telegraphed, every choice is legible. Position for flanks. Push the heat budget. Shoot their limbs off before they shoot yours off. Between missions, you're in the hangar: strip parts, swap arm weapons, tune each machine to your plan.

Across 3 missions, a rookie lance earns its legend by surviving **VEX** — the ace who hunts them. The duel is personal.

Built with vanilla JS. Playable on desktop and mobile browsers — touch-first, single-tap everything.

## Controls

- **Tap/click a unit** — select it; its move range lights up
- **Tap a highlighted tile** — move there
- **Tap an enemy in range** — target it; pick which limb to hit
- **End Turn button** — tap again to confirm
- **Intent telegraphs** — enemy attacks show before they fire; read them, then move
