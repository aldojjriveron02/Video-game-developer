Validation — the Kleenex protocol, confirmed as a real named method. One-time use per test subject; it validates first impressions and initial learning curve. The steps: bring in a fresh player who's never seen the game, observe their first interaction, record where they struggle, and do NOT reuse the same player for this type. The Black Box variant: give the player the game with no instructions beyond basic controls, observe their natural approach, note where assumptions differ from design intent. And the Fundamental Attribution Error corrective: when a tester fails, designers instinctively blame the tester — correct by assuming the game might be at fault.
