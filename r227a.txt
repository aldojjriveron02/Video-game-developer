Full report is in your Drive. The jam mobile findings. The ideal LD game "can be played right in the browser, in a few minutes, alone." Judging is by fellow participants, one to five stars across nine categories plus "Coolness" — and a game that needs a phone in-hand, a specific orientation, or a download gets skipped; friction costs ratings. The freeze mechanics: both events add a one-hour Submission Hour after the forty-eight or seventy-two hours to package and submit; porting may legally happen after the hours, but "the longer you wait, the less time participants will have to play your game" — late mobile work directly trades ratings. After submission only certain bug fixes are allowed, no new features, and you post a short change log. The process wisdom: aim for a personal deadline two hours before the real deadline; do a warm-up submission earlier in the jam; ship an MVP first and never be caught without a submittable build; submit early and often; make it browser-playable since most players won't download; capsule art and screenshots take time, budget for them. For our project the synthesis is explicit: mobile is NOT-ready and LD is desktop-first. The mobile freeze is therefore a containment problem, not a feature problem — lock what's done, prevent regressions, reserve jam hours for the theme. Validation — the final mobile checklist. From abratabia's mobile browser guide: boot and full play in the mobile browser, playable start to finish; controls never cover the action area; audio works — unlock after first tap, mute works, no autoplay before gesture; text readable at phone scale, UI inside safe areas; no horizontal or vertical scroll, page body fixed; frame time under sixteen milliseconds; test on a real device — iOS Web Inspector via desktop Safari's Develop menu; viewport meta present. From supagamesai's mobile-first lesson: start, play, pause, and restart without a keyboard; all tap targets sixty-four pixels minimum; pointer-to-game coordinate conversion correct after resize; readable in portrait and landscape, or explicitly request one orientation.
