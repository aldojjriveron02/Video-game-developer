Our twenty-minute thermal soak is this game's equivalent; the freeze checklist should require the soak on the FINAL slice build, on the iPhone itself, since sustained load triggers iOS thermal throttling that short benchmarks miss. WebAudio: MDN best practices — one AudioContext per page, resume on user gesture; freeze checklist must re-verify unlock plus ducking plus the locked mix on iOS Safari, not just desktop. The techniques — freeze-safe perf polish, nothing that restructures code. Fixed-size, prewarmed, bounded pools with a free list — free list gives O(1) alloc and free; freeze-safe validation means asserting the pool never grows past cap under M3 VEX duel load. Offscreen pre-render of repeating primitives: render static grid and arena elements once to an offscreen canvas, blit per frame. Integer coordinates — Math dot floor on drawImage, never scale inside drawImage, cache pre-scaled sizes. alpha false on getContext when no transparency is needed. Batch canvas calls, minimize state changes, avoid shadowBlur and per-frame text rendering. DPR cap around two and draw-call ceiling in the low hundreds as measured targets. GC discipline: near-zero steady-state allocation in the hot loop; split hot and cold paths. Timestep guards from Glenn Fiedler's "Fix Your Timestep": clamp frameTime — his example clamps at zero-point-two-five seconds — keep fixed-dt simulation with an accumulator so behavior is frame-rate independent, and leave headroom so the sim can catch up from spikes. What to avoid — the perf freeze traps. The spiral of death: variable timesteps without a max-step clamp — when the sim can't keep up, catch-up steps pile up and it falls further behind; never enter the jam with unclamped delta time; a background tab or thermal dip during a long rating session will trigger it. Optimizing without profiling — late "optimization" commits that touch the renderer during freeze are the classic jam perf regression source. Last-minute render-target changes: DPR bumps, enabling shadows or transparency, or new full-screen effects after lock. Feature work in the perf window. Shipping the perf HUD at full cost — the HUD itself consumes frame budget; JAM_BUILD should keep it dormant.
