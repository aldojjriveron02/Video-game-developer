# Mecha Protocol — Playtest Script (one page)
**Version:** 2026-10-14 · **Build:** prototype (unlisted itch.io page) · **Method:** think-aloud + silent observation + telemetry + post-survey

## Before the session
- Recruit: 4–5 naive testers per wave (r/playtesters, r/playmygame, tactics Discords). Max 1–2 friends-of-friends. Screen: tactics experience + device (iPhone/desktop mix).
- Send: unlisted build link + "play when ready, ~20 min" + consent to record (if recording).

## Intro (read verbatim)
> "I'm testing the game, not you — there are no wrong answers and nothing you do can be wrong. Please say everything you're thinking out loud as you play. I can't answer questions or help while you play; just do what feels natural. You can stop anytime."

## Task
> "Play the game as you would at home. Start whenever you're ready."

## During (say nothing except)
- Silent >20s → "What are you thinking now?"
- Tester asks a question → reflect back: "What do you think it does?"
- NEVER: help, hint, defend, or ask "do you find this easy?"

## Watch for
Hesitation · mis-taps · surprise · repeated/undone actions · skipped tutorial text → later confusion · accidental heat-zone crossings · restarts · hangar first-click behavior · time-to-first-action.

## After (FFWWDD — Schell)
1. What was your favorite moment or interaction?
2. What was your least favorite moment or interaction?
3. What was one thing you wanted to do that you couldn't?
4. If you had a magic wand, what's the one thing you'd change?
5. How would you describe your strategy?
6. How would you describe this game to a friend?
7. (indirect fun probe) Did you want to keep playing after the first mission?

NEVER ask: "Did you like it?" / "Would you buy it?" / "Is it a good idea?" (Mom Test).

## After they leave
1. Review telemetry FIRST (mission funnel, restart rate, per-lesson drop-off, undo/heat usage, TFA) — words must not anchor the numbers.
2. Then read survey answers. Numbers = frequencies; words = feelings and confusion.
3. Log: problem, severity (catastrophic = blocks progress), wave, tester ID. One fix batch per wave.
