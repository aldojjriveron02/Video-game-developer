The jam save findings — and the big one first. itch.io's iframe sandbox. Admin leafo: itch must run games in an iframe on a different domain for security — untrusted JS — plus site-locking against aggregators that steal games to put ads on top. The consequence: when the player blocks third-party storage — Chrome with third-party cookies blocked, or Safari on iPhone with default "Prevent cross-site tracking" ON — localStorage is denied or silently degraded in the embed, even though it works on localhost. The Fracas 3 devlog: "if you block third-party cookies in Chrome, HTML5 games hosted on itch.io are denied access to LocalStorage and SessionStorage, even though it still works if you're testing using localhost." The itch forum thread: users "lost hours of progress." This is why Export-primary is the correct posture — and on iPhone 17, the storage-degraded path is not an edge case, it's the mainstream path. The Survival Guide budgets four of forty-eight hours for publish and submit and warns "you definitely don't want to be looking for an exporter during Submission Hour." MDN documents navigator.storage.persist — one line, may reduce eviction. Validation. bugnet: validate save data integrity BEFORE persisting so corrupt data is never written; never let corrupt data overwrite a good backup; on load, validate before using — checksums distinguish corruption from tampering; backups must verify before use. Migration: every save carries a version; chain stepwise v1 to v2 to v3, then validate AFTER migration.
