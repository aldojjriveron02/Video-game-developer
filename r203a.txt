The jam a11y findings. Ludum Dare has NO accessibility gate in its rules — seventy-two hours, theme at start, three weeks of voting, judging one to five stars across Innovation, Fun, Theme, Graphics, Audio, Humor, Mood, Overall. Web entries "can't require fullscreen." So accessibility is a ratings advantage, not a checkbox — and LD's own ideal ("can be played right in the browser, in a few minutes, alone") is itself an accessibility win: low friction, no installs, short session. The numbers from the Game Accessibility Guidelines: fifteen percent of the general population disabled, rising to twenty percent amongst casual gamers; eight percent of males have red-green color deficiency. itch.io now has structured accessibility metadata — set via the Classification tab in the project dashboard, and games can be filtered by it. GAG's "Inform" step: list key accessibility considerations on the game page and notify review outlets — a11y features "can net good review scores and awards." The precedent: Global Game Jam's 2014 accessibility diversifier saw a record one hundred sixty-nine games account for disabled players, and jam games have been cited as blowing "the accessibility efforts of most AAA games out of the water" in under forty-eight hours. Validation — the final-pass protocol. The Xbox Accessibility Guidelines v3.2: twenty-three guidelines, each shipping a ready-made audit structure — Goal, Overview, scoping questions, Background, key areas to target, implementation guidelines, player impact. They're explicitly "a checklist to validate the accessibility of their titles," not a compliance hammer. The GAG six-step process: Familiarise, Evaluate and plan, Prioritise and schedule, Implement, Inform, Review and learn — with the prioritisation formula Reach times Impact times Value, and tiers from Basic ("easy to implement, wide reaching") up through Advanced.
