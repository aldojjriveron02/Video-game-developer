The jam feel findings. The gamestack checklist orders the final pass: control, then simulation, then forgiveness, then juice — "Don't juice a verb that doesn't yet feel right." Test-for criteria: input-to-pixel latency at or under fifty milliseconds for action at sixty fps on target hardware; a "mute plus no particles plus no screen effects" test — the stripped simulation must already feel acceptable before polish. Hit-stop from a per-event table, three to ten frames scaled to magnitude, with a cap on cumulative hit-stop per second. Trauma-based screen shake — accumulate and decay. Input buffer one hundred to two hundred milliseconds; coyote time four to six frames. All UI value changes eased, never linear; audio-video sync within fifty milliseconds; audio identifiable with the screen off; UI hover one to two hundred milliseconds, press squish under a hundred, transitions two to five hundred. The JUICE PACK starter numbers: hit-stop forty to eighty milliseconds, squash ten to twenty percent, pitch variance plus or minus ten percent. Kao's inverted-U: summed feedback should sit in the Medium to High band — Extreme hurts as much as None.
