LD60 starts Friday October sixteenth at six PM — seventy-two hours, teams fine, plus a one-hour Submission Hour after the deadline for packaging. Bug fixes after the deadline are allowed — no new features, short changelog required — the emergency lane for a mobile-specific hotfix. Submission model: you upload screenshots to LD, but host binaries elsewhere — browser game on itch.io is the canonical route. The rules: "The ideal Ludum Dare game can be played right in the browser, in a few minutes, alone." Judging is one to five stars, and mobile friction — broken audio, layout under the notch — hits Fun and Overall directly for mobile raters. itch's official HTML5 docs: enable "Mobile Friendly" in embed settings — on mobile the page forces "Click to launch in fullscreen"; without the flag, visitors see a warning the game may not work. ZIP limits: one thousand files max, five hundred megabytes total, two hundred per file, paths under two hundred forty characters, UTF-8, case-sensitive; relative paths only; browsers are click-to-play so audio stays muted until interaction — a first-gesture unlock is mandatory. Validation — the real-device protocol from Abratabia and Lacombe. One: test on the LOWEST-END device you intend to support, not just the flagship — "A game that runs at a smooth sixty fps for the first three to five minutes can start to throttle on real hardware." Two: remote debugging — Android via chrome inspect, iOS via Safari Web Inspector — that's how the perf HUD numbers get verified against reality. Three: touch input — respond on touchstart not touchend, no double-tap requirement, swipe accuracy, prevent pull-to-refresh, verify on BOTH iOS and Android, test interruptions — calls, notifications, tab switches.
