Part 2 of 3. Thermal specifics: mobile SoCs are passively cooled; the game launches at peak GPU clock and throttles after a few minutes. Flagships sustain only fifty-five to seventy percent of peak GPU performance. Design the render budget around sustained, not peak: "use only sixty to seventy percent of GPU peak capacity." Single most effective power reducer: render at about seventy percent of native resolution. Soak protocol: ten to fifteen-plus minutes continuous, log every frame. NSProcessInfo dot thermalState is NOT accessible to web content — infer throttling from frame-time degradation instead. Three-signal instrumentation: MDN's longtask API covers main-thread tasks over fifty milliseconds via PerformanceObserver — but it's Baseline Limited, supported in Chrome and Edge, NOT in Firefox or Safari. Our iPhone target lacks it, so the re-measure must use an rAF-delta fallback: count frames over fifty milliseconds as the jank surrogate, which our perf HUD can already do. The techniques — last-mile polish. MDN's canvas guide: pre-render repeated sprites to offscreen canvas; avoid floating-point coordinates; never scale images in drawImage — cache each size once; layered canvases for background, game, UI; getContext two-d with alpha false; avoid shadowBlur; prefer clearRect; render only screen differences. Gaffer on Games "Fix Your Timestep": fixed-dt accumulator with render interpolation; clamp the frame delta to avoid the spiral of death; "leave some headroom." Meow Mountain's postmortem — a thirteen-kilobyte js13k game: music synthesized via AudioWorklet on a separate thread; SFX via wave functions; sprites as base-thirty-two strings. Our WebAudio synth design already mirrors this pattern. What to avoid. Lopes's gotchas: high-refresh-rate screens — rAF fires at one-twenty or one-forty-four hertz; anything tuned for sixty hertz breaks — iPhone seventeen ProMotion is one-twenty hertz, so our loop must not assume sixty. Browser monoculture — don't assume Chrome; test Safari explicitly. speechSynthesis voices may be undefined — guard it.
