## A. Jam tutorial
- 3-10 min per game; opening minute decides. Idris (feel → loop → return).

## B. Validation
- Cronin (1-on-1 sync; free). Idris (5 silent players; >3s hesitations; top 3 fixes).
- Instrument (time to action; per-beat completion; failure recovery).

## C. Techniques
- Hodent (learning-by-doing; max 3 items; teach WHY; no punishing states).
- Pine (invisible: affordances, skill gates, demonstrative, trial-error, contextual).
- Prompts (moment of need; one idea; non-modal; dismissible+repeatable).
- Pacing (introduce→practise→combine; never two at once).

## D. What to avoid
- Decision before understanding. Explaining before playing. Text walls.
- Unskippable friction. Hand-holding. Punishing learners. Two mechanics at once.
