Accessibility Statement — Mecha Protocol

Last updated: October 15, 2026
Standard: WCAG 2.2 (self-assessed)
Compliance status: Partially compliant

Our Commitment

Mecha Protocol is committed to being playable by as many people as possible. We have built accessibility features into the game from the start, and we document our limitations honestly below.

Supported Accessibility Features

FeatureWhat it doesWho it helps
Reduced MotionAutomatically disables screen shake when your OS has "Reduce Motion" enabled. No action needed.Motion sensitivity, vestibular disorders
Shake Intensity3-tier control (High / Medium / Off) in settings. Persists across sessions.Motion sensitivity
Team Shape MarkersTeams identified by ▲/▼ shapes, not color alone. Works under all color-vision deficiencies.Color blindness
Text ScalingMultiple text-size tiers in settings. UI remains usable at 200% browser zoom.Low vision
Keyboard NavigationAll menus and dialogs fully keyboard-operable with visible focus indicators.Motor impairments, screen reader users
Screen Reader SupportMenus, dialogs, and HUD controls announce name/role/state. Combat events narrated via live regions.Blindness, low vision
High ContrastUI meets WCAG AA contrast ratios. Focus indicators are 3px solid with glow.Low vision
Pause AnywhereGame can be paused during tutorials, briefings, dialogue, and combat.Cognitive, attention

Known Limitations (Honest Gaps)

LimitationReasonWorkaround
Tactics grid not keyboard-navigableCanvas grid is pointer/touch-only by design (touch-first for iPhone). Arrow-key cursor not implemented.Use touch or mouse for grid. All menus around the grid are keyboard-accessible.
No per-layer volume slidersMaster volume + mute only. Independent music/SFX/UI sliders not implemented.Use master volume. Mute available.
Japanese text needs native-speaker reviewJA translations are functional but have not been reviewed by a native speaker.Jam build is English-only. JA available post-jam after review.
