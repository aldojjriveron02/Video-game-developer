Jesse Fish: after reaching submittable state, "show it to as many people as you can" and make a fix or no-fix decision on each note before sleeping. The techniques — Jazudu's last-mile numbers. Screen shake zero-point-one to zero-point-three seconds, two to five pixel offset, randomized direction each frame, ALWAYS include a settings toggle for motion sickness. Particles five to fifteen per burst max, palette-matched, slight randomness. Hitstop two to four frames on attack connect. White flash one to two frames on damage. Pitch randomize zero-point-nine to one-point-one. Every player action gets at least one sound. Never linear UI motion — ease everything; score counts up, health lerps, menus slide. Layer big-moment sounds — boom plus debris plus bass rumble. Priority: "Start with screen shake, particles, and sound. Those three alone will transform how your game feels." The feedback-budget pattern: a debug overlay summing live feedback load — emitters, peak shake pixels, hit-stop per second, sfx layers — trigger every event at once and verify the peak stays under the High ceiling.
